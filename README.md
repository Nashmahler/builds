# Weekly Builds

One AI build a week, picked from what AI companies are hiring people to solve.

**Live page:** https://nashmahler.github.io/builds

An agent I built reads ~4,500 open roles at 20 AI companies four mornings a week and surfaces the problems those companies keep hiring for. Each week I pick one I can build in about 6 hours, ship it, and post what worked and what broke.

| Week | Build | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Agent Answer Grader: grades an AI support agent's answers against policy and flags anything wrong or invented | In progress |
| 1 | Build Radar: the agent that picks what to build next | Shipped (code private) |
| Earlier | [AI Market Intelligence Agent](https://github.com/Nashmahler/ai-infra-intel-brief) | Shipped |

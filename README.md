# Weekly Builds

One AI build a week, picked from what AI companies are hiring people to solve.

**Live page:** https://nashmahler.github.io/builds

An agent I built reads ~28,000 open roles at 200+ companies four mornings a week and surfaces the problems those companies keep hiring for. Each week I pick one I can build in about 6 hours, ship it, and post what worked and what broke.

| Week | Build | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | [Agent Answer Grader](https://github.com/Nashmahler/agent-answer-grader): grades an AI support agent's answers against policy, then measures the grader against my own labels ([results](https://nashmahler.github.io/agent-answer-grader/)) | Shipped |
| 2 (bonus) | [Account Research Agent](https://github.com/Nashmahler/account-research-agent): researches any company and drafts sales outreach openers, with every claim checked against its source | Shipped |
| 1 | Build Radar: the agent that picks what to build next | Shipped (code private) |
| Earlier | [AI Market Intelligence Agent](https://github.com/Nashmahler/ai-infra-intel-brief) | Shipped |
